About JobPortal

JobPortal is a modern recruitment platform that connects job seekers with employers. Companies create professional profiles, post jobs and manage applicants. Job seekers build a profile, upload a resume and apply with a single click.

For job seekers

Create a professional profile, search and filter jobs and track every application.

For employers

Showcase your company, post openings and move candidates through your hiring pipeline.

Safe & private

Passwords are hashed and resumes are only visible to employers you apply to.
